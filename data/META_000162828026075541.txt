In connection with recent lawsuits filed by various shareholders in the Court of Chancery in Delaware, which have been consolidated under the action captioned In Re Meta Platforms, Inc. Class C Reclassification Litigation, Consol. C.A. No. 2026-1413-KSJM (the &#8220;Shareholder Litigation&#8221;), asserting claims challenging the recapitalization of Meta Platforms, Inc. (the &#8220;Company&#8221;) with a trial scheduled to begin April 19, 2027, the Company has agreed that it will not issue any shares of Class C common stock, whether through a dividend or otherwise, during the pendency of the pre-trial and trial proceedings in the Court of Chancery. In furtherance of this, Mark Zuckerberg, the Company&#8217;s founder, Chairman, and Chief Executive Officer, granted a waiver to the Company of the requirement in the Founder Alignment Agreement, dated October 20, 2026, between the Company and Mr. Zuckerberg, that the dividend of Class C common stock previously declared by the Company&#8217;s Board of Directors (the &#8220;Stock Dividend&#8221;) be paid by no later than the fifth business day following the effectiveness of the Company&#8217;s amended and restated certificate of incorporation. Such waiver will expire on the earlier of (i) the Company&#8217;s failure to pay the Stock Dividend within a reasonable period following resolution of the Shareholder Litigation that does not prohibit or enjoin the payment of the Stock Dividend; (ii) a final, non-appealable resolution of the Shareholder Litigation that prohibits or enjoins the payment of the Stock Dividend; and (iii) June 30, 2027, if the Stock Dividend has not been paid on or prior to such date. Because the Company will not launch its previously announced at-the-market program until the Class C common stock begins trading, it does not expect to launch the at-the-market program until after the Stock Dividend is paid.
